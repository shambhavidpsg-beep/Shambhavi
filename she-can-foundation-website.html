Who We Are

Global Vision, Local Action

We are She Can Foundation, a non-governmental organization registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860, dedicated to empowering women and creating a more equitable society.

We provide support, resources, and training to women in communities across the globe, working closely with local organizations, governments, and communities to ensure that our programs are effective and sustainable.

Our Journey

Growing Stronger Every Year

Foundation Started

She Can was founded by a group of individuals who shared a vision of creating a world where every woman has the opportunity to thrive — born from a desire to break down barriers for women in communities across the globe.

Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can volunteer with She Can?
Anyone aged 16 and above who shares our values of empowerment, equality, and inclusion is welcome. We have roles for students, professionals, retirees, and everyone in between.
Do I need prior experience?
Not at all! We provide training and mentorship for all volunteers. What matters most is your passion for making a difference. Skills can be taught; commitment comes from the heart.
How much time do I need to commit?
We offer flexible volunteering — from 2 hours a week to full-time engagement. You can start small and grow with us. We have weekend programs, online roles, and event-based volunteering.
Can I donate instead of volunteering?
Absolutely! Financial contributions fund scholarships, workshops, and community programs. Every rupee goes directly toward empowering women. You can also donate skills, equipment, or time.
Is She Can Foundation registered?
Yes. She Can Foundation is a government-registered NGO under the Indian Society Act, 1860. You can view our certificates at shecanfoundation.org/our-certificate. We rely on the support of individuals, corporations, and organizations to achieve our vision.
