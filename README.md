# 🪄 The Wand Chooses the Wizard — A Moving Staircase

> **A magical, scroll-driven journey through Hogwarts where the staircase moves as you explore each floor.**

Welcome to **The Wand Chooses the Wizard**, an interactive Harry Potter–inspired web experience built around the idea of a **magical moving staircase**.

Instead of navigating a website through traditional menus, users travel through **30 magical floors**, with each scroll revealing a new section, challenge, concept, or piece of the Hogwarts-inspired world.

The project combines **storytelling, animation, interaction, and frontend development** to create an immersive digital experience.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏰 30 Magical Floors

Explore **30 different floors**, starting from the **Ground Floor** and progressing through themed magical sections.

Each floor reveals new content as the user scrolls through the experience.

### 🪜 Scroll-Driven Moving Staircase

The central staircase is designed as a **3D visual element** that rotates and moves according to the user's scroll position.

The staircase acts as the visual representation of progressing through Hogwarts.

### 🕯️ The Great Hall

The journey begins in the **Great Hall**, introducing the magical atmosphere and inviting the user to begin their exploration.

### 🧪 Potions Dungeon

Enter the **Potions Dungeon**, where an interactive ingredient-and-cauldron mechanic adds an element of exploration and experimentation.

### 📚 Grand Library

The **Grand Library** contains a searchable archive, allowing users to search through the available floors and discover specific sections.

### 🦁 Gryffindor Wing

A collection of floors inspired by **Gryffindor**, focusing on courage, determination, confidence, and boldness.

### 🦅 Ravenclaw Wing

The Ravenclaw-inspired floors explore ideas around **knowledge, intelligence, logic, and curiosity**.

### 🦡 Hufflepuff Wing

The Hufflepuff section represents themes of **loyalty, patience, dedication, and hard work**.

### 🌌 Astronomy Section

The Astronomy-inspired floors create a more cosmic atmosphere, bringing stars, exploration, and wonder into the experience.

### 🧙 Headmaster's Tower

The journey concludes with a **riddle-based interaction** in the Headmaster's Tower.

Users have to solve the riddle to unlock the final interaction.

## ⚡ Interactive Experience

The website isn't designed to behave like a conventional static webpage.

The main interaction is **scrolling**.

As the user progresses:

```text
Scroll
  ↓
Staircase rotates
  ↓
Floor changes
  ↓
New magical section appears
  ↓
New interaction / content revealed
  ↓
Continue exploring Hogwarts
```

The staircase and progress rail work together to visually communicate the user's position within the journey.

---

## 🧭 Navigation System

A fixed **progress rail** appears alongside the experience.

It displays:

* Current floor number
* Current floor name
* Overall scroll progress

This allows users to understand where they are within the **30-floor journey**.

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

The experience has been designed to adapt to different screen sizes.

On smaller screens:

* The staircase scales down
* The progress rail becomes more compact
* Floor names are hidden when necessary
* Content spacing is adjusted
* The layout becomes more mobile-friendly

The project also includes a **reduced-motion fallback** for users who prefer less animation.

## 🪄 The Concept

The idea behind the project was to turn **website scrolling into a journey**.

Instead of:

> *Click → Open Page → Read → Go Back*

the experience becomes:

> **Scroll → Move the staircase → Discover a floor → Interact → Continue the journey**

The staircase therefore becomes more than a visual decoration — it acts as the **navigation metaphor of the entire website**.
